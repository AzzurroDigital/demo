The house

Quiet, green, and a short drive over the bridge.

The apartments are in our family house in Sveti Vid Miholjice, above Malinska on the island of Krk.

Downstairs there is a courtyard under a shade sail, with a barbecue and flower boxes. Around the house, a garden with olive trees and palms. Upstairs, a covered terrace looks out over the bay.

You can park on the property.
