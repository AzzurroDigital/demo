Awnings

Manufacture and installation of awnings for terraces, balconies, yards, hospitality venues and business premises. We offer different awning types and systems, adapted to every space and purpose.

Folding arm awnings

The folding arm awning is the most commonly installed model. It is made of powder-coated aluminium profiles, with stainless steel connecting parts. Its purpose is protection from the sun.

Vertical awnings

The ideal solution for closing in loggias. Affordable, simple, and good sun protection. The fabric is lowered by a crank handle or a motor, via a switch or remote control.

Guide-rail awnings (Atika)

An awning model with side guides that covers larger areas and is widely used in hospitality. It is wind resistant, and with double-impregnated fabric it also protects from rain.

Dome awnings

The ideal solution for covering protruding loggias and protecting them from sun and rain. Made of powder-coated aluminium profiles holding acrylic fabric or PVC.

Fixed awnings

For permanently covering a chosen area. Elegant, and well suited to printing advertising on the canopy and valance.

Pergolas

Pergolas are motorised awnings adapted to enclose all kinds of spaces: hospitality venues, terraces and open areas such as roof terraces. With a multi-bay system a large area can be covered. They are wind resistant and protect from rain.

Glazing option available

Bioclimatic pergolas

The bioclimatic pergola has a roof and structure of movable aluminium louvres with simple, clean lines. With the louvres raised to an angle of 135 degrees the space is ventilated; lowered and closed, they fully protect the covered area from sun and rain.

Louvres open up to 135°

Sun sails

Sun sails combine versatility, elegance and effective sun protection. On some models a sensor and motor rotate the sail to follow the sun. Fixed sails cover an area permanently, while motorised models can be fitted with a wind sensor that closes them automatically under heavy load.

Pool covers

Pool covers protect the pool through the winter. They are made of opaque PVC, black on the underside to prevent algae growth. The customer chooses the colour of the top side, usually blue or green.
